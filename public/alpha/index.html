SETUP · ABOUT FIVE MINUTES

Set it up in five steps

  1. 1

    Put it on your Home Screen

    1. Tap Open Curbside at the bottom of this page, in Safari.
    2. Tap Share (), then Add to Home Screen.
    3. From now on, open Curbside from its icon, not from Safari.
    1. Tap Open Curbside at the bottom of this page, in Chrome.
    2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
    3. Open Curbside from its icon.

    The icon and Safari keep separate data, so do the rest in the app you opened from the icon.

  2. 2

    Start your list, and save the code

    Tap START A NEW LIST. Curbside shows your list code, eight letters and numbers. Tap SHARE and send it to yourself (Notes, or a text), then SAVED.

    There are no accounts or passwords: the code is your list. Lose it and nobody can get the list back. Settings () always shows it.
  3. 3

    Turn on flight alerts

    Settings () → List code and alerts → TURN ON, and say yes when the phone asks. Curbside then buzzes this phone itself when a flight moves, with what to do and your new times. Tap SEND A TEST to see one arrive.

    On an iPhone this only works in the app opened from its Home Screen icon (step 1).

  4. 4

    Import dispatch's sheet

    1. In Outlook, open dispatch's email and tap the PDF.
    2. Tap Share (), then Save to Files.
    3. In Curbside, tap the file icon () at the top and pick the PDF.
    1. Open dispatch's PDF from the email.
    2. Tap Share, then Curbside. That's it.

    A line under the header says what each file did. When dispatch re-sends a run, import the new sheet too: the newest print replaces the old one, and your corrections stay.

  5. 5

    Drive with it

    Open the run when you start. What you're doing now is at the top, with ROUTE IN MAPS under it for every stop still ahead. Message a passenger with WHATSAPP or TEXT, and tap a flight, door, time or leg that's wrong to fix it. Dispatch's sheet is still the plan: Curbside is there to help you follow it.

TELL US

Feedback, and good to know

Sending feedback

Settings () → SEND FEEDBACK. The notes that help most say:

  • what you tapped,
  • what you expected,
  • what you saw instead.

Your note goes with the app version, never your list code. Please leave out passengers' names and phone numbers.

Good to know

  • It's a test. Dispatch's sheet is the plan; when it matters, check a flight on the airline's app too.
  • Flight checks are rationed on a free plan: a few per flight. If it says paused, times are the last check's or as printed.
  • Your data: the PDF is read on your phone. The run is stored under your code and deleted 7 days after it ends (change it in Settings).
