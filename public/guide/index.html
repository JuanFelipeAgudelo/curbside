How Curbside works

Curbside turns dispatch's assignment PDF into your run: every stop, every passenger, and their flights, checked while you drive. It never changes dispatch's plan. When a flight moves, it shows what that means beside dispatch's own times.

1 · ONCE

Put it on your Home Screen

iPhone

  1. Open this address in Safari (not another browser).
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. From then on, open Curbside from its icon.

Android

  1. Open this address in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
On an iPhone, the icon and a Safari tab keep separate data. Set up your list in the app you opened from the icon, and use that one.
2 · ONCE

Save your list code

Tap START A NEW LIST. Curbside gives you a list code of eight letters and numbers. There are no accounts and no passwords: the code is your list, and it is the only way back to it on a new phone or after this one's data is cleared.

  • Save it right away: SHARE it to your notes or text it to yourself, or COPY it. Then tap SAVED.
  • Settings () shows it any time.
  • On another phone, choose USE THIS CODE and type it in. Both phones then show the same list.
Lose the code and the list is gone: nobody can look it up for you. Don't share it with anyone who shouldn't see your runs.
3 · EVERY SHEET

Import dispatch's sheet

iPhone (Outlook)

  1. Open dispatch's email and tap the PDF.
  2. Tap Share, then Save to Files, and save it.
  3. In Curbside, tap the file icon at the top () and pick the PDF.

Android

  1. Open the PDF from the email, tap Share, then Curbside.
  2. Or save it, then tap the file icon () in Curbside.

Laptop

Open the same address and drop the PDF anywhere on the page.

The sheet is read on your phone. A line under the header says what each file did: a new run, an update, or why a file couldn't be read. Several at once is fine, and with no signal they wait and go when it's back.

When dispatch re-sends a run, import the new sheet: the newest printed copy replaces the older one, and your own corrections stay. The line says how many things dispatch changed, or Reprinted when a sheet was only printed again. The run's card says UPDATED and the run lists WHAT DISPATCH CHANGED until someone on your list taps OK.

4 · ON THE ROAD

Read a run

One meaning per colour

Brass: here

The stop or leg you're on now, and nothing else.

Blue: a time moved

A flight is running early or late against its schedule.

Red: a problem

Something to check: a flight change that moves your clocks, a number missing from the sheet, or a new sheet that disagrees with your correction.

Green: done

A stop that's behind you, a leg you've ticked, and a passenger you've ticked as messaged.

Violet: Governing Body

A GB passenger: double the margin, and louder alerts.

Two clocks at every airport stop

PARKED AT10:45p

Be in the lot before the first flight lands: 15 minutes before touchdown, 30 for a GB passenger. For a drop-off, it's the check-in deadline: 2 hours before a domestic departure, 3 hours international.

LEAVING11:45p

When you can expect to leave with the last passenger: 45 minutes after landing on a domestic flight, 1 hour 45 international (bags and customs).

Both follow each flight's latest estimate. Dispatch's own times stay on the stop, beside them.

Your corrections

Tap a flight number, a door or a time to correct it. The three dots on a passenger's card open the rest for that passenger: their phone number, their greeting, not coming. CHANGE THIS STOP, at a stop's foot, adds a passenger dispatch phoned in or says the stop isn't happening. Your value is marked as yours and dispatch's stays visible. A passenger who isn't coming stays on the run, greyed out, with an UNDO that never runs out.

What Curbside couldn't read from a sheet is listed at the foot of the run. A flight it couldn't name, like an airline it has no code for, has FIX: type the flight (WS1680) and, unless you untick it, your list remembers the airline's code. Later sheets that name that airline get their flights filled in, marked LEARNED; tap one to change it for that passenger. Settings → Airlines lists what your list learned, to forget one, and the ones every driver has.

When dispatch re-sends a run

What dispatch changed since the copy your list last OK'd is marked where it happened: the old time, vehicle, door or flight struck beside or under the new one, NEW on a stop, passenger or leg the older sheet didn't have, and what the new sheet dropped left where it was, dashed and struck: NOT ON THE NEW SHEET. A passenger dispatch moved says MOVED HERE and where they were.

After OK the marks stay on the stops until dispatch's next change. Where you'd set your own value, yours stands: the list says what dispatch changed, and the stop asks you to keep yours or use the sheet.

Messaging passengers

MESSAGES FOR … on the list is everyone you collect on your next day of runs. WHATSAPP and TEXT open a draft of your own message, filled in for that passenger. On a run, each passenger's card has the same two beside their number, as WA and TEXT. Write yours in Settings → Messages: one per kind of trip (the tag on dispatch's sheet picks it), and your name to sign it. For someone travelling alone, Curbside asks once whether it's Brother, Sister or just the name.

[FROM] and [TO] are places a passenger knows: Warwick, Fishkill, Patterson, or the airport. [DOOR] is where you'll wait at the pickup, as dispatch printed it: A-res WRK, Baggage Claim.

A bracket left in the draft, like [FLIGHT], means the sheet didn't say: fill it in before you send. On the day of the run the chat opens empty. Curbside never sends anything, you do, so tick each passenger off yourself.

If a passenger's plan changes after you ticked them (dispatch re-sent the sheet, or you corrected a time, a door or a number), they count as left again: MESSAGED 6:12p · CHANGED SINCE in red, with what changed and what it was. Their draft now starts with "Update:". Send it, then tick them again. A flight running late doesn't do this: only the plan does. Someone a re-sent sheet added is marked NEW until you tick them.

Staying up to date

While Curbside is open it asks your list every minute whether anything changed: a sheet imported on another phone, a correction, a flight check. A change shows within a minute or two, without pulling down.

On the list of runs, a run's card says when one of its flights is off schedule (UA1503 26M LATE, in blue) and how many passengers you messaged before their plan changed (1 TO MESSAGE AGAIN).

Flight alerts on your phone

Settings → List code and alerts → Alerts on this phone → TURN ON, and say yes when the phone asks. From then on Curbside itself buzzes this phone when a flight moves: what to do first, then the flight and your new times beside dispatch's. No other app is needed. On an iPhone it works in the app opened from its Home Screen icon, not in Safari. Each phone turns its own on.

SEND A TEST sends one alert the same way and says whether it went to this phone, so you know before a real delay has to prove it. Nothing arrives? Check that the phone lets Curbside send notifications (the phone's Settings, Notifications, Curbside). If alerts ever stop on this phone, the list of runs says so at the top.

Used the ntfy app before? It is still sent every alert, but its free service often turns Curbside away. Once alerts are on here you can unsubscribe there, or you may get some twice. The same page says how many flight checks are left this month; every driver shares them.

Settings → Save a copy gives you a file of your runs, edits and settings: your own backup. It never holds your list code; keep that apart.

Removing a run from this phone

At the bottom of a run, REMOVE FROM THIS PHONE takes it off your list of runs, your messages and your route. It leaves a dashed line where the run was, with UNDO, until the run is cleaned up with the other finished ones. Importing its sheet again also brings it back.

Only this phone: the run stays on the list for anyone else who uses your list code, and its flight alerts still arrive.

5 · SHUTTLES AND DUTIES

Windows and their legs

A stop dispatch prints with no address and nobody to carry, like a shuttle, Dispatch or Floater, is a window: a dashed band with dispatch's label and hours. The timed lines in dispatch's notes are its legs, each with its time and a box to tick.

  • RIGHT NOW reads the leg you're on, from its time until the next leg's time or your tick. Curbside never ticks one for you.
  • A starred time, like 9:50*, stays as dispatch printed it: what it hangs on is yours to judge.
  • Tap a leg's words to fix its time or words. Yours shows beside dispatch's, and USE DISPATCH’S puts it back. NOT HAPPENING there greys the leg out, with an UNDO; the one under the legs is for the whole window.
  • + ADD A LEG for one dispatch phoned in. It's marked as yours.

Timed lines under an ordinary stop work the same way. Your fixes reach every phone on your list; ticks stay on this one.

6 · ANY RUN

The route in Maps

ROUTE IN MAPS, under RIGHT NOW, shows every stop still ahead, in dispatch's order, starting from where you are. Change it there before it goes to Maps:

  • The arrows move a stop up or down; the cross leaves it out (ADD BACK returns it).
  • + ADD A STOP adds one of your places, a sheet's or an airport.
  • CHANGE beside the start starts somewhere other than where you are.
  • NEXT STOP ONLY sends just the next one.

Then APPLE MAPS or GOOGLE MAPS. Your order is kept on this phone for that run until it ends, marked YOURS; DISPATCH’S ORDER puts it back. The run itself keeps dispatch's order. Tick "go straight to Maps" to skip the stops next time; Settings → Maps turns it off again. On a shuttle, the stops are the places its legs name, from the leg you're on.

When the car's space says where it waits, the route starts there until the run is under way: a space starting FKL-NBD is at NCA, and the first stop says "collect at NCA". Google Maps takes 10 stops at a time. Pick Google only if its app is on the phone: in a browser, Google takes just a few stops (NEXT STOP ONLY always works).

Places

Maps needs an address for every code. Codes a sheet prints with an address are known already. A code only the notes use says, for example, NTH NEEDS AN ADDRESS: ADD it beside the stop (and a name, if you like), or leave that stop out.

  • All of them are in Settings → Places: YOURS, FOR EVERY DRIVER, then FROM DISPATCH’S SHEETS.
  • FOR EVERY DRIVER are the common ones, already set for you. The same goes for airlines in Settings → Airlines.
  • EDIT one of those, or one from a sheet, to use your own address instead.
  • Your places belong to your list: every phone on it shares them, so each one is added once.

A place you add or an airline you teach is also sent to the person who looks after Curbside (the code, name and address, not who you are or any passenger), so a place everyone needs can be set once for every driver.

7 · ON THE FREE PLAN

Flight checks

Curbside runs on a free flight-data plan with a small monthly allowance, so it checks each flight a few times instead of all the time:

  • an arrival: 3 hours and 45 minutes before landing, and 15 minutes after;
  • a departure: once, 3 hours before.

When the month's allowance runs low you'll see “Flight checks running low”: only the check before landing still runs. When it's used up, “Flight checks paused until …”: times are the last check's, or as printed. Confirm a flight on the airline's app or the airport's board before you rely on it.

8 · ANY TIME

Tell us what broke

Settings () → SEND FEEDBACK. Anything helps: something wrong, something confusing, something you wish it did. The most useful notes say what you tapped, what you expected, and what you saw.

Your note goes to the app's owner with the app version, never your list code. Please leave out passengers' names and phone numbers.
